Behind the rink.

This player loads NHL26 Shoresy Edition — Standard 1.07, with the smoother NOSHO/maple-leaf pregame background, the BROdude broadcast logo and twelve clean-language pregame conversations. Jory Jordan interviews Shoresy in four new scripts; Anik/Jay and Remy/Benny retain all eight existing conversations, including both originals. A conversation is chosen randomly for each pregame; Jory interviews appear in Bulldogs matchups, and exclude interview guest Shoresy from the hot/cold player mentions. In 50% of eligible Bulldogs games, Shoresy's hockey portrait introduces the five-skater brawl with “Let's give 'em the lumber, boys!” Every Bulldog wins and celebrates before the original faceoff resumes. The Jims retain maximum fighting ratings. The ROM header credits the NHL 2026 ’94 Edition base to JKline3 & von Ozbourne. Original game and character material belong to their respective owners. This is an unofficial fan project.

The arena photograph is by Jfvoll, Wikimedia Commons, under CC BY-SA 4.0. The adapted background replaces the Wolves sign with Bulldogs artwork and applies cropping, brightness and Genesis color conversion.

The browser emulator is EmulatorJS 4.2.3, by Ethan O’Brien and the EmulatorJS contributors. It uses RetroArch compiled to WebAssembly and the Genesis Plus GX core.
