Behind the rink.

This player loads NHL26 Shoresy Edition — Standard 1.02, with eight pregame conversations: the original plus three new conversations for each announcer pair, all without swearing. One conversation is chosen randomly for each pregame. In 50% of eligible Bulldogs games, five skaters pair off and win a cosmetic pregame brawl with staggered celebrations before the opening faceoff. The Jims have maximum fighting ratings. Its ROM header credits the NHL 2026 ’94 Edition base to JKline3 & von Ozbourne. Original game and character material belong to their respective owners. This is an unofficial fan project.

The browser emulator is EmulatorJS 4.2.3, by Ethan O’Brien and the EmulatorJS contributors. It uses RetroArch compiled to WebAssembly and the Genesis Plus GX core.

The emulator files are hosted with this site, with two local controller-detection fixes. Readable modified source is included in the repository. Saves stay on your device unless you export them. There are no accounts or cloud saves. Hosting providers may log requests as part of serving the site.
